# AD 2026-TR-0105 — Engine Oil Pressure Transmitter Replacement (TRAINING SAMPLE, not a real FAA AD)

## Applicability
All airplanes in the training fleet with the affected oil pressure transmitter part number.

## Unsafe condition
The transmitter can give a false low oil pressure indication, which may cause an unnecessary in-flight engine shutdown, or hide a real low pressure condition.

## Required actions
1. Within 1,200 flight hours, replace the oil pressure transmitter on each engine with the improved part.
2. If a low oil pressure indication occurs, do the AMM 79 troubleshooting procedure before replacing the transmitter.

## Compliance
Record the transmitter part and serial numbers installed.
