# AMM 32 — Brake Wear and Nose Gear Shimmy (TRAINING SAMPLE, synthetic)

## Brake wear pin at limit
1. Measure the wear pin length with the parking brake set.
2. If the pin is flush with the housing or shorter, replace the brake assembly before further flight.

## Nose gear steering shimmy
1. Check tire pressure on both nose wheels.
2. Inspect the shimmy damper for fluid leaks and the torque links for free play.
3. If free play is above limits, replace the bushings.
