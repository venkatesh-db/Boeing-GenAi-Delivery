# AD 2026-TR-0102 — Bleed Air Duct Clamp Replacement (TRAINING SAMPLE, not a real FAA AD)

## Applicability
737 MAX 8 airplanes, engine bleed air ducts between the pre-cooler and the wing leading edge.

## Unsafe condition
V-band clamps on the bleed duct can loosen, causing hot air leaks, duct overheat warnings, and possible damage to wiring nearby.

## Required actions
1. Within 90 days, replace the V-band clamps with the improved part.
2. If a "Duct overheat" or "Bleed trip off" message occurs, inspect the clamps for looseness before further flight.

## Compliance
Record the clamp part number and date of replacement in the aircraft records.
