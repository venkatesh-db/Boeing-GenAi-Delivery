# AD 2026-TR-0103 — Hydraulic Pump Case Drain Filter Inspection (TRAINING SAMPLE, not a real FAA AD)

## Applicability
787-9 airplanes with engine-driven hydraulic pumps.

## Unsafe condition
Metal particles in the pump case drain filter can indicate internal pump wear. Undetected wear can cause loss of a hydraulic system.

## Required actions
1. Inspect the case drain filter for metal particles every 600 flight hours.
2. If metal particles are found, or the hydraulic pressure fluctuates, replace the pump before further flight.

## Compliance
Record the filter inspection result and any pump replacement.
