# AD 2026-TR-0101 — CFM56-7B Fan Blade Inspection (TRAINING SAMPLE, not a real FAA AD)

## Applicability
This training directive applies to 737-800 airplanes with CFM56-7B engines installed.

## Unsafe condition
Reports of fan blade root cracking found during shop visits. Cracking that is not detected could lead to fan blade release, engine vibration, and damage to the airplane.

## Required actions
1. Within 500 flight cycles after the effective date, do an ultrasonic inspection of all fan blade roots on both engines.
2. If engine vibration (N1 vibration) above 3.0 units is reported in cruise, do the fan blade inspection before further flight.
3. Replace any blade with a crack indication before further flight.
4. Repeat the inspection at intervals not exceeding 1,500 flight cycles.

## Compliance
Record the inspection in the maintenance records, including the AD number, method, and results, as required by 14 CFR 43.9.
