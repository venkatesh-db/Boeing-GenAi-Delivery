# AMM 72-00-00 — Engine High Vibration Troubleshooting (TRAINING SAMPLE, synthetic)

## Indication
Engine vibration (N1 or N2) indication above 3.0 units in cruise, or a crew report of airframe vibration.

## Possible causes
1. Fan blade damage or cracking (see AD 2026-TR-0101 for 737-800 / CFM56-7B).
2. Fan imbalance after blade replacement.
3. Faulty vibration sensor or wiring.
4. Bearing wear (often together with rising EGT and oil consumption).

## Procedure
1. Download the engine vibration data from the flight data recorder for the last 10 flights.
2. If vibration is above 3.0 units on more than one flight, do a borescope inspection of the fan and the high pressure compressor.
3. Do a visual inspection of the fan blades for nicks, cracks, or erosion.
4. If no damage is found, check the vibration sensor resistance and the wiring connector.
5. If vibration is recurring together with an EGT margin trend down, consider an engine removal for shop visit.

## Return to service
Do an engine ground run and confirm vibration is below 2.0 units at all power settings.
