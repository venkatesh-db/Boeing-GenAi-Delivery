# 14 CFR Part 121 — Air Carrier Maintenance Program (plain-language training summary)

This is a simplified summary for training. Always use the official regulation text.

- 121.363: The air carrier is primarily responsible for the airworthiness of its aircraft, including compliance with airworthiness directives.
- 121.367: The carrier must have a maintenance program that ensures work is done according to its manual and that aircraft released to service are airworthy.
- 121.373: The carrier must have a continuing analysis and surveillance system (CASS) to monitor the effectiveness of its maintenance program. Recurring defects, such as repeated engine vibration reports, should be analysed and corrected.
- 121.380: The carrier must keep records of the current status of applicable airworthiness directives, including the method of compliance.
