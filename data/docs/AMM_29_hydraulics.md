# AMM 29 — Hydraulic Pressure Fluctuation (TRAINING SAMPLE, synthetic)

1. Check the reservoir fluid level and the reservoir pressurisation.
2. Inspect the pump case drain filter for metal particles (787-9: see AD 2026-TR-0103).
3. Check for external leaks at the pump and pressure module.
4. If pressure fluctuates by more than 300 psi with a normal fluid level, replace the pump.
