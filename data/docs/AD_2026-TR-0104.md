# AD 2026-TR-0104 — Main Landing Gear Axle Inspection (TRAINING SAMPLE, not a real FAA AD)

## Applicability
777-300ER airplanes, main landing gear axles.

## Unsafe condition
Corrosion under the axle chrome plating may lead to axle cracking and loss of a wheel.

## Required actions
1. At the next tire change, or within 1,000 flight cycles, do a detailed visual and eddy current inspection of each axle.
2. Repair or replace any axle with corrosion or cracks before further flight.

## Compliance
Record the inspection and the axle serial numbers inspected.
