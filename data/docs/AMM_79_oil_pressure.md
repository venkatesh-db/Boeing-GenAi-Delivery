# AMM 79 — Low Oil Pressure Indication (TRAINING SAMPLE, synthetic)

1. Check the oil quantity. Service if low.
2. Compare the indicated oil pressure with a calibrated test gauge during an engine ground run.
3. If the test gauge reads normal, the transmitter is faulty: replace it (see AD 2026-TR-0105).
4. If the test gauge also reads low, check the oil filter bypass indication and inspect the oil filter for metal.
